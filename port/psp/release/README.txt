MvC2 PSP
========

1. Drag your Marvel vs. Capcom 2 (USA) PS2 .iso onto the builder
   (or open the builder and choose the .iso).
2. It makes a folder called MVC2 next to the .iso.
3. Copy MVC2 into PSP/GAME/ on your memory stick.
4. Play. The .iso is not needed on the PSP.

Do not share the built EBOOT: it contains game data from your disc.
