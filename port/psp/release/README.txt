MvC2 PSP
========

You need: your own PS2 disc image of Marvel vs. Capcom 2: New Age of Heroes
(USA, SLUS-20486): .iso, .bin/.cue, .cso, .zso, or a .zip of one of these;
and a PSP-2000 or later with custom firmware
(or PPSSPP). The PSP-1000 does not have enough memory. About 200 MB free on
the memory stick.

1. Drag your disc image onto the builder (or open the builder and choose it).
   First time only, see "Opening the builder" below.
2. It checks your disc and makes a folder called MVC2 next to the disc image.
3. Copy MVC2 into PSP/GAME/ on your memory stick, so you have
   PSP/GAME/MVC2/EBOOT.PBP.
4. Play: Game > Memory Stick on the PSP (PPSSPP: open MVC2/EBOOT.PBP).
   The disc image is not needed on the PSP.
5. Do not share the built EBOOT: it contains game data from your disc.

Your disc image is only read; nothing is uploaded anywhere.
Settings: PSP/GAME/MVC2/settings.txt, created on first run.
More: https://github.com/mrbrainz/mvc2-psp
